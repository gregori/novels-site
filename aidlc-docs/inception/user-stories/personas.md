# Personas

## Persona 1: The Reader

**Name (archetype)**: Reader

**Who they are**: A fan of Japanese webnovels/light novels who discovers fan translations online. Reads primarily on a phone, often in short sessions (commute, before bed). May not be a native English speaker but is comfortable reading English fan translations.

**Goals**:

- Find new/ongoing translated titles that match genres they like
- Keep up with new chapter releases for titles they follow
- Read comfortably for long stretches without eye strain
- Occasionally discuss chapters with other readers

**Frustrations (with the old Blogspot setup)**:

- Hard to browse titles/chapters in order
- No way to filter by genre/category
- Fixed typography, poor mobile reading experience
- No lightweight way to know when a new chapter drops

**Technical comfort**: Low to medium — expects a simple, familiar reading-site experience (like other fan-translation aggregators), not a technical tool.

## Persona 2: The Translator (Site Owner)

**Name (archetype)**: Translator

**Who they are**: The site's sole content author and maintainer — translates Japanese webnovels/light novels into English as a hobby/learning exercise, in spare time.

**Goals**:

- Publish new chapters with minimal friction (write Markdown, push, done)
- Keep each title's metadata (status, synopsis, categories, credits) easy to maintain
- Give proper credit/disclaimer to original authors
- Avoid maintaining any backend, database, or server infrastructure
- Have a calm, presentable site that reflects a hobby project, not a commercial platform

**Frustrations (with the old Blogspot setup)**:

- Blog format doesn't fit a "titles with chapters" structure
- Limited control over layout/theming
- No simple version-controlled authoring workflow

**Technical comfort**: High — comfortable with Git, Markdown, and GitHub, wants the publishing workflow to be "edit Markdown, commit, push."
