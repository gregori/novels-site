---
chapterTitle: 'Closing Time'
publishDate: 2025-11-16
---

They turned the sign at nine, but nobody moved to leave. Mika made tea nobody had asked for; Sato pretended to finish the register twice. Rin sat by the window and watched the street lights come on, one at a time, like the town was closing its own eyes.

It was, everyone silently agreed, exactly the right amount of nothing to happen tonight.
