---
chapterTitle: 'The Clerk Who Fell'
publishDate: 2026-01-05
---

The ledger fell from Wren's hands before the world did.

One moment he was reconciling tax receipts by candlelight; the next, the floor of the counting house dissolved into a grey nothing that tasted, absurdly, of static. He did not remember falling. He remembered landing — face-first, in mud, under a sky the wrong color for any hour he knew.

Somewhere above him, iron struck iron, and a voice that was not speaking his language became, without his permission, perfectly understandable.

"Another one," it said. "Get up, ledger-man. The throne doesn't wait for people who count things."
